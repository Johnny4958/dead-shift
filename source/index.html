HOW TO PLAY

Survive as many rounds as you can. Shooting zombies earns points. Spend points on doors, guns on the walls, the Mystery Box, perk machines, and base building. Zombies break the boards off the windows. Stand at a window and hold USE to nail them back for points. Open the Parking Lot door to reach the car and the open ground for building. Turn on the power in the Garage to wake up the perk machines and The Forge.

TIPS

Headshots pay more. Knife kills pay the most. The Mystery Box costs 950 and moves after a few uses. Fat zombies soak up bullets, skinny ones sprint, and glowing green ones burst into poison gas when they die, so keep your distance. Walls you build block zombies until they chew through them, and spikes shred anything that walks over them.
